# Style and Store Research: yalebulldogblue.com

Source: https://www.yalebulldogblue.com (researched 2026-10-17)

## Brand
- **Name:** Yale Bulldog Blue by Campus Customs
- **Tagline:** "Officially Licensed Merchandise" (shown above the nav)
- **Slogans:** "Casual comfort, classic Bulldog pride." · "Add a touch of Bulldog pride" · "Sip in style, the Bulldog way" · "Big Spirit, Big Yale" · "Hats Off"

## Visual style
- **Colors:** Yale blue and navy on white. The site doesn't list exact colors; Yale Blue is `#00356B`.
- **Header:** Logo, a dropdown mega-nav (Clothing / Accessories / Home / Collections) and a cart icon. On mobile the menu opens as a full-page list.
- **Home page:** A full-width hero banner, then themed carousels: "Show your residential college pride!", "Shop Bulldog Blue", "Big Spirit, Big Yale" and "Hats Off".
- **Product card:** Image, a sale badge ("Save X%"), title, price, a short fabric or fit note and a "Quick shop" / "Choose options" button.
- **Calls to action:** "Shop All", "View product", "View cart" and "Checkout"
- **Footer:** Links to the Refund Policy, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Contact pages, plus "© 2026 Yale Bulldog Blue by Campus Customs".

### Returns and exchanges
- 30 days from the ship date. Items must be unworn and unused, with the original tags still attached.
- Final-sale and custom items (such as Custom Alumni products) can't be returned or exchanged.
- If the store made the error, it pays return shipping. If the customer changed their mind, the customer pays (insured, trackable shipping is recommended).
- Original shipping costs aren't refunded. Refunds take 2–10 business days to arrive.
- **To start a return:** ship the item back with any carrier, then email the tracking number to orderdept@campuscustoms.com.

### Catalogue categories
- **Clothing:** T-shirts and tops, jackets and sweatshirts, activewear, polos and bottoms, for men, women, youth and infant/toddler.
- **Accessories:** Hats, neckwear, jewelry, pet items, bags, decals and magnets, and keychains.
- **Home:** Decor, pennants and banners, drinkware, books, and office supplies.
- **Collections:** Alumni, Class of 2026 and 2030, residential colleges, sports, relatives (Mom, Dad and so on), and graduate and professional schools.
- **Example prices:** Residential college quarter-zips are $59.99 and crewnecks are $39.99. Brooks Brothers Big Yale apparel is also sold.
